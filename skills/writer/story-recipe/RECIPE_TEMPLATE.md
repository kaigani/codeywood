## Beat spine

Sized to the genre's unit (chapters / episodes / acts / seasons). Each Beat
cell is 2–4 **causal sentences** — a character's choice + what it
irreversibly changes (Law 2) — ending with the two wiring lines. The
emotional target is an **audience emotion**, never a theme demonstration.

Tag grammar: `E<emitting-beat>-<concrete-artifact-noun>` — emitter-prefixed,
naming a story object (a lie, a debt, a wound, a tool, a secret, a body).
Banned nouns: consequence, choice, cost, constraint, aftermath, event, outcome.

| Position | Beat | Emotional target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | {2–4 causal sentences keeping the beat's content and imagery.} **Needs:** — **Sets up:** E1-{artifact} (→E{n}) | {what the audience feels at this moment} |
| ... | {...} **Needs:** E{n}-{artifact} **Sets up:** E{n}-{artifact} (→E{m}) | ... |

Wiring rules (all must hold — see methodology Step 7): no orphan beats;
no unfed setups; front half carries forward obligations; final two beats
consume ≥3 distinct earlier emissions (≥2 character choices); costs are
paid later; flaws fire as choices.

If the genre needs multiple resolutions (e.g. a 36-chapter novel with both chapter beats and 4-act movements), include both tables; wire the finer-grained one.
