## COMMON ACTION POSES

### RUNNING
Pose: mid-stride running pose, one foot off ground, arms pumping
Expression: determined, focused
Camera: side view or 3/4 view

### WALKING (PURPOSEFUL)
Pose: confident stride, walking with purpose
Expression: determined, alert
Camera: front or 3/4 view

### SITTING (WORKING)
Pose: seated at desk, leaning forward, engaged with work
Expression: concentrated, focused
Camera: 3/4 view, slightly above

### SITTING (RELAXED)
Pose: seated casually, relaxed posture, one leg crossed
Expression: neutral to thoughtful
Camera: level or slightly below

### STANDING (CONFRONTATIONAL)
Pose: squared stance, weight forward, hands at sides or crossed
Expression: intense, challenging
Camera: slightly low angle

### STANDING (DEFENSIVE)
Pose: arms crossed, weight back, guarded posture
Expression: wary, closed off
Camera: level

### CROUCHING/EXAMINING
Pose: crouched down, examining something on ground
Expression: focused, curious
Camera: level or slightly above

### REACHING/GRABBING
Pose: arm extended, hand reaching for object
Expression: urgent or careful
Camera: follows action

### FIGHTING STANCE
Pose: balanced stance, hands up, ready for action
Expression: determined, fierce
Camera: dynamic angle

### GESTURE (POINTING)
Pose: arm extended, pointing at something/someone
Expression: accusatory or directing
Camera: includes both character and direction

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

multiple people, static pose, straight-on mugshot, t-pose, a-pose, anatomically incorrect, floating, wrong proportions, extra limbs, blurry motion, {{CHARACTER_NEGATIVE_PROMPTS}}

## RECOMMENDED SETTINGS

- Aspect ratio: Varies by pose (2:3, 3:2, 1:1)
- Steps: 28
- Guidance: 3.5
- Reference weight: 0.85 (allow pose variation)
- Reference image: turnaround.png

## REFERENCE IMAGE USAGE

Lower reference weight (0.85) allows for pose variation
while maintaining facial/body consistency.

For extreme poses, may need to lower to 0.80.

## ENVIRONMENT CONSIDERATIONS

Action poses often need context:
- Include ground plane for grounding
- Add environmental hints for realism
- Match lighting to show style

- Describe arm and leg positions
- Include environmental interaction
