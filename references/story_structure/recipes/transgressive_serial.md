# Transgressive Body-Tech Descent (Serialized)

## Slot
Adult animated serialized sci-fi — a single season following ONE protagonist's descent. A lonely misfit gains forbidden access to a hidden system of power that enters through the body (cybernetic augmentation, a signal, a demon, a drug, an upload, an occult bargain), and the power that solves their weakness slowly converts them into something the system was built to consume. The serialized single-protagonist counterpart to `transgressive_anthology` — same engine, one continuous arc instead of standalone episodes plus a rating-host. Pipeline-native (10-episode season). Commercial neighborhood: the AKIRA / Cyberpunk: Edgerunners / Devilman Crybaby / Texhnolyze shelf.

## Corpus
Read at the SERIES level, not the episode level.

Long-form single-arc entries — the spine:
- *AKIRA* (Otomo)
- *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners* (Trigger)
- *Devilman Crybaby* (Yuasa)
- *Texhnolyze* (Hamazaki)
- *Serial Experiments Lain* (Nakamura)
- *Genocidal Organ* (Murase)
- *Ghost in the Shell: Innocence* (Oshii)
- *Belladonna of Sadness* (Yamamoto)
- *Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within* (Sakaguchi — body-horror / posthuman anchor)

Anthology corpus, mined for the per-story content engine:
- *Love, Death + Robots*, *Heavy Metal*, *Junji Ito Maniac*, *Aeon Flux*, *Superjail!*

(Tonal range: Edgerunners' tragic-romantic through Devilman Crybaby's apocalyptic-erotic through Lain's dissociative-metaphysical to Belladonna's painterly transcendence. Same engine, opposite registers.)

## Emotional contract
**Desire + mutation + systems horror + ecstatic collapse.** The viewer watches a beautiful nightmare reveal the truth of the world — and the truth changes the body. A damaged outsider gains forbidden power; the power feels like freedom; the power is the system getting *inside* them. The catharsis is awe, dread and grief at once: we watched a person be converted into fuel, and it was gorgeous. The takeaway is not a lesson — it is a single image that feels sacred, obscene and irreversible.

Where `romantasy` is *forbidden desire → power awakening → dangerous intimacy*, this is *forbidden perception → bodily transformation → systemic annihilation*. The pleasure is **overload**: sensory, sexual, moral, technological, metaphysical.

## Trope vocabulary

**Core (the engine):**
- **Forbidden Transformation** — The protagonist gains access to something they should not have. It must be BOTH empowerment AND violation. Only empowerment = superhero fantasy; only violation = passive horror. The genre needs both: every use solves the immediate weakness and moves them further from the human shape they began with.
- **The System Eats the Body** — A large system converts flesh, identity, sex, memory, or spirit into fuel. The world's ideology must become physical — capitalism becomes cybernetic self-destruction (Edgerunners), psychic power becomes expanding meat (AKIRA), identity becomes signal (Lain). The body is where the truth appears.
- **The System Built a Place For You** — The horror is not "they want to kill me." It is: the system wants the protagonist because they are *useful* — a weapon, a host, a sacrifice, a data node, a messiah, a reproductive vessel, an evolutionary bridge. It built a place for them before they knew what they were.
- **Eroticized Doom** — Desire is not decorative; it opens the gate. Sex, longing, envy, hunger, worship become the transformation mechanism. The protagonist confuses being used with being chosen.
- **Beauty in Collapse** — The visual register at the moment of ruin is glorious. A body blooms into machinery; a corpse becomes divine; a digital void turns serene. Repulsive and gorgeous in the same frame.

**Supporting tropes (stack 3-5 across the season):**
- **The Overload Sequence** — A set piece where perception breaks: fast cuts, body distortion, screaming color, fractured montage, gore and sexual metaphor and machine diagrams. The genre pays off its visual promise here.
- **The Mirror Already Crossed** — The protagonist glimpses the Body-Horror Mirror; the denial ("that won't be me") is the audience's certainty.
- **The City as Organism** — Architecture behaves like anatomy: elevators, pipes, cables, towers reading as veins, guts, bones, wombs, tumors. The City wants too.
- **Unreliable Reality** — Dream, memory, network, simulation, hallucination and physical event become hard to separate. The true horror is loss of ontological certainty.
- **The Angel / Demon Ambiguity** — The transcendent figure is never purely holy or evil: God looks like a machine, a demon speaks truth, an AI develops longing, a savior triggers apocalypse.
- **Youth as Weapon** — A young, emotionally volatile person carries the world-ending force. The immaturity is the engine, not an accident.
- **Power Feels Like Freedom (first)** — Before the cost lands, the genre lets the power feel *good* — a bully beaten, an elite space entered, a body finally seen. The audience must understand why the protagonist keeps going.

**Anti-tropes (the genre does NOT do these):**
- No clean escape. The protagonist never walks away human and intact; innocence is never restored. (Consumption has modes — see Variation rules — but escape is not one of them.)
- No moral lesson. The takeaway is the image, not a teaching.
- No protagonist with a quest. The engine is internal hunger growing from a wound — not an external goal.
- The System is not defeated cleanly. It is exposed, ruptured, or reborn — the nightmare is structural.
- No empowerment without violation, and no violation without empowerment. A transformation that is only one of those has failed the slot.

## Visual register

Transgression is a VISUAL contract — the concept-art phase (Step 7) draws from this section directly.

- **Color** — radioactive neon, bruised purple, surgical white, arterial red, sickly green, oily black, flesh pink, religious gold. Saturation runs hot; the palette itself should feel unsafe.
- **Texture** — chrome against skin, wet concrete, cables routed like veins, fluid and static and ash, decaying luxury, medical surfaces, viscera rendered as ornament.
- **Motion** — sudden stillness then violent eruption; bodies stretching past anatomy; looping ritual violence; hallucinated montage; transformation staged as choreography, not accident.
- **Design principle** — every object should read as becoming one of four things: flesh, machine, signal, or myth. A frame trending toward none of them is set-dressing — cut it.

## Conformity rules
- The forbidden transformation must be BOTH empowerment AND violation — every use solves the weakness and costs the human shape.
- The world's ideology must become PHYSICAL — the system converts flesh / identity / memory / spirit into fuel, and the body is where that truth is shown. Off-screen consequence is a failure of the form.
- The system wants to USE the protagonist, not merely kill them — it built a place for them.
- No clean escape, no innocence restored. The protagonist is always consumed; the ending is never simple restoration.
- The protagonist carries an ordinary human wound the appetite grows from — the consumption must cost something the audience recognized as human.
- Adult animated register — hand-drawn or stylized 3D, never live-action photorealism. The aestheticization of the body is non-negotiable.
- At least one Overload Sequence in the first four episodes and one at the climax.

## Wiring tests
- Delete-test: no episode can be removed without a later episode breaking.
- The climax consumes at least two character choices from distinct earlier episodes.
- Every named flaw fires as a choice whose consequence is visible in a later episode.
- Every relationship the cast table says moves shows its early state AND its changed
  state as on-screen actions.
- Every cost charged is paid: a later episode is harder in a named way because of it.

## Variation rules
- **The world** — the Meat City (dense decaying urban organism: AKIRA, Texhnolyze, Edgerunners), the Abstract Hell (closed symbolic space, dream logic: Superjail!, Belladonna), the Networked Void (reality dissolving into signal: Lain, Innocence), or the Contaminated Planet / Species (infection, demonic emergence, alien memory: Devilman, Spirits Within).
- **The forbidden access** — cybernetic augmentation, a signal, a demon, a drug, a consciousness-upload, an occult bargain, military language-tech, alien contamination.
- **The transformation's consequence shape** — decay, fusion, fission, replication, recursion, dissolution.
- **The mode of consumption (how the season ends)** — *beautiful annihilation* (death, sublime ruin: Edgerunners), *posthuman ascension* (the protagonist becomes something past human — a god, a network, a swarm: AKIRA), or *dissolution* (they disperse into signal, data, myth: Lain, Innocence). Always consumed; the form is the dial.
- **The Catalyst's relationship** — lover, demon, assassin, rebel, machine-ghost; fuses with / betrays / dooms / saves the protagonist.
- **Tonal register** — operatic (Devilman Crybaby), clinical (Texhnolyze), orgiastic (Belladonna), kinetic (Edgerunners), dissociative (Lain).
- The wiring's edges are fixed (the ordinary wound invites access; desire repeats the paid bodily limit; surveillance enables harvest; the Mirror's scar and hidden map become the final rupture method) — every system, symptom, appetite, and mode of consumption varies freely.

## Caveats
- Serialized single-protagonist counterpart to `transgressive_anthology`. Use THIS recipe for pipeline runs (the pipeline produces serialized 10-episode seasons); the anthology recipe is the spec for a future `anthology_phase`.
- The transgressive engine triggers Gemma's safety training specifically. The brief's hardest pillars (sex, operatic body horror, eroticized doom) get progressively scrubbed at each pipeline step. The conformity rules enforce them as the FLOOR; the editor's commercial-accessibility clause must NOT translate transgression into restraint for a transgressive brief. (See `feedback_commercial_accessibility.md`.)
- Audience-tolerance band is narrow and culturally variable: push too far and it reads as exploitation; pull back and it loses the engine into prestige adjacency.
- New recipe — not yet wired into `data/persona_pool.json` affinities, so it is currently reachable only via `--recipe transgressive_serial` or the baseline full-library draw. Assign it to ~3-5 transgressive/horror-leaning personas (e.g. Meret Still, Corin Bastide, Saffron Loop) if it should be head-writer-selectable.
